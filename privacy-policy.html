Privacy Policy

Federal & Sindh Pension Calc

Effective date: September 2026

About this app

Federal & Sindh Pension Calc is a pension calculation and planning tool. It provides estimates and pension increase schedules based on information entered by the user.

Information processing

Pension details entered into the app are used to calculate pension estimates, gross and net pension values, commutation amounts, and increase schedules. We do not operate a personal account system and do not send pension details to our own servers.

Storage

Information may remain temporarily on the device while the app is being used. We do not sell, rent, or share pension information with third parties.

Advertising

If advertising is enabled, the app may use Google AdMob. Google may process device identifiers, advertising data, approximate location, and ad interaction information according to its policies.

Read Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13.

Accuracy

The app provides estimates for planning purposes. Verify all results against official pension rules and records before making administrative or financial decisions.
