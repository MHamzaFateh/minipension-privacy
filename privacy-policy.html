Privacy Policy

Federal & Sindh Pension Calc

Developer: Mini Federal

Effective date: November 12, 2026

About this app

Federal & Sindh Pension Calc is an independent pension calculation and planning tool. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by a government. This policy describes the current ad-free Android app.

Information accessed and used

You may enter dates of birth, appointment, retirement or death; employment grade and pay stage; salary and allowance amounts; and pension calculation settings. The app uses these details to calculate pension estimates, commutation amounts and pension increase schedules locally on your device.

No registration or user account is required. The app does not require your name, CNIC, bank account details or payment information. Please do not enter such information.

Data collection and sharing

The app does not transmit your calculation inputs or results to the developer or to third-party servers. We do not sell, rent or share your pension inputs or results.

The current app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not access the Android advertising identifier.

Secure data handling

Calculation inputs and results are processed in the app's memory on your device. The app does not write pension cases to a database or upload them to a server. It relies on Android's application isolation to restrict other applications' access to its memory.

The privacy-policy website is served over HTTPS. No pension inputs are included in the website address. No security measure provides absolute protection. Protect your device with a screen lock, keep its software updated, and avoid sharing screenshots containing sensitive information.

Data retention and deletion

Inputs and results remain temporarily in memory during the app session. Switching to another app may leave this session in memory. You can clear or replace editable inputs, or force-stop the app through Android settings to end its process and discard the current calculation session.

The app does not maintain saved pension cases or server-side copies of your calculation data. There is no user account to delete. Screenshots you create yourself remain on your device until you delete them.

Support communications

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request and investigate reported problems. Our email provider processes these communications. Please use fictional examples instead of sending sensitive personal or financial records.

Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve your request and meet applicable legal obligations. You can request its deletion by emailing us. This retention applies to support messages, not to calculation inputs, which we do not receive.

External services

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit this page, GitHub may process connection information, including your IP address, under its own privacy policy. Your pension inputs are not sent to GitHub by the calculator.

GitHub Privacy Statement

Google Play and Android may independently process installation, diagnostic and service information under Google's policies and your device settings. Google Play may make app-quality reports available to the developer. These are separate from the pension inputs processed locally by the calculator.
